---
name: research
description: Use when a question needs independent investigation against primary sources and a cited durable report.
---

Before writing, read `../_shared/artifact-policy.md` and follow it.

Delegate the bounded question to the configured `researcher` role or the client's equivalent when independent delegation is available. Otherwise perform the same investigation directly.

Follow each material claim to the primary source that owns it: official documentation, source code, specifications, or first-party APIs. Separate verified facts from inference. Write one concise Markdown report with citations near the claims they support, then return its path and a short conclusion.
